MISSION BRIEFING

What is Lunar Ice Watch?

A browser-based prototype for exploring lunar south-pole candidate sites and the evidence behind them.

Why the lunar south pole?

Permanently shadowed regions can remain extremely cold because sunlight rarely reaches their floors. That makes them important targets when screening for possible water-ice resources.

Multiple evidence signals

Compare thermal conditions with radar indicators and terrain context instead of relying on a single measurement.

From site to route

Once a candidate is selected, the Map and Traverse views provide a visual way to inspect the area and simulate a rover path.
